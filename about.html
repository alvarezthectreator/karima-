About Cuisine Efficace

Distinctive flavours.

Cuisine Efficace is a Lagos-based catering service offering exquisite French cuisine with an African twist. We cater private and public events, corporate occasions and offshore operations, bringing a thoughtful approach to food and service.

Every gathering has its own character. We begin by understanding the occasion, the guests and the kind of experience you want to create, then shape the catering around that brief.
