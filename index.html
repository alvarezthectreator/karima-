Hospitality with

A few useful details

Before we
begin.

Every brief is different. Here are a few things to know before you enquire.

What kinds of events do you cater?

Private celebrations, corporate functions, executive hospitality and offshore catering. Share your event brief and we can discuss the right approach.

Can the menu be tailored to our event?

Yes. The menu and service style can be discussed around your occasion, guest profile and event requirements.

Do you provide offshore catering?

Offshore catering is one of Cuisine Efficace’s stated services. Please include the location, duration and operational requirements in your enquiry.

How do we request a proposal?

Message us on Instagram with your date, venue or location, estimated guest count and the service you need. The team can follow up with next steps.
